A new neighborhood gathering place

We’re just getting started—and we’re here to stay.

Seminole Bus Station Cafe is a new local restaurant with a simple ambition: serve comforting food, make everyone feel welcome, and become a place our community can count on for years to come.

We want to grow alongside Seminole. That means showing up for our neighbors, supporting local makers and businesses whenever we can, and creating a friendly place for travelers, families, and the people who call this community home.
